TOURNAMENT RULES

All participating teams must read and follow the rules before entering the match.

01. GENERAL RULES

  • Each team must consist of exactly 4 players.
  • All teams must join the room on time.
  • Players must provide screenshots or recordings whenever requested by the admin.
  • All teams must correctly follow the instructions given by DEVIL'S ARENA organisers.

02. GAME SETTINGS & DETAILS

  • Character Skills: OFF
  • Gun Attributes: ON
  • Random Store: ON

03. ALLOWED GUNS

Only the following weapons are permitted during the match:

Desert Eagle
UMP
M10
MP4P
M1887
Woodpecker

STRICTLY PROHIBITED:

  • Chipped M10 is strictly prohibited.
  • Using any weapon not included in the allowed weapons list is strictly prohibited.

04. LOADOUT RULES

  • Only Team Booster is allowed.
  • Only ONE player per team is permitted to use Team Booster. The other three players must remove their loadouts.
  • Using auto-revive through a loadout is not allowed.
  • If a player receives auto-revive by mistake while using Team Booster, that player must be eliminated using the zone.
  • Normal revive is allowed.
  • Using any other loadout and activating it during the game will result in DIRECT DISQUALIFICATION.

05. GAME RULES

PROHIBITED:

  • Rooftop usage is prohibited.
  • Steps usage is prohibited.

ALLOWED:

  • Gloo jumping is allowed.
  • Gloo breaking is allowed.
  • Single-container play is allowed.
  • Zone pack is allowed.

06. STRICTLY PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

  • Hacking or cheating.
  • Using panels or unauthorised third-party tools.
  • Exploiting game bugs or glitches.
  • Teaming with opponents.
  • Using prohibited weapons.
  • Abusive behaviour towards other players or admins.

Any violation may result in a warning or direct disqualification, depending on its severity.

07. REPORTING SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITIES

  • If a team suspects an opponent of cheating, using panels or showing abnormal movement, the team or team leader must report it to the host.
  • The complaint must include valid proof, such as a screenshot or game recording.
  • Complaints must be submitted within 5 minutes after the match ends.
  • The team accused of the violation must immediately join the official DEVIL'S ARENA Discord server when instructed by the organisers for verification.
  • Teams must cooperate with the admins during the investigation.

08. TEAM RESPONSIBILITY

  • Every team is responsible for knowing all tournament rules.
  • Team leaders must share the complete rules with all their teammates.
  • Every player is responsible for following the rules throughout the tournament.
  • Not knowing the rules will not be accepted as an excuse for rule violations.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

All teams must follow the official DEVIL'S ARENA tournament rules. Rule violations may result in warnings or direct disqualification. The organisers reserve the right to review reported incidents and take appropriate action.
